Season & Playoffs

  • 20 regular-season matchups, one week each. No tiebreaker for tied matchups; no home-field advantage; no bonus wins.
  • Top 8 of 12 make the playoffs: quarterfinals, semifinals, and championship, one week each.
  • Seeding tiebreaker: head-to-head record. Bracket is not reseeded between rounds.
  • A consolation ladder runs for the teams that miss out. Teams out of the running for 7th place are locked from transactions.
